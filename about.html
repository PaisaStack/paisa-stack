Why Paisa Stack exists

Most personal finance content in India is written either for complete beginners or for people who already speak the language of finance fluently. Between a Chartered Accountant's technical fluency and a salaried professional's genuine numeracy, there's a real, underserved audience — and that gap is exactly what Paisa Stack is built to close, using the same rigor we'd apply to a paying client's return, just written in plain language.

What you'll find here

Free calculators for the moments that actually confuse people — tax regime choice, salary structure, property purchases, loans, and long-term savings schemes. Short, practical ebooks. Free downloadable trackers. And paid help — a direct consultation, or done-for-you filing services — for anything that needs a real conversation instead of a calculator.

What this isn't

This site is a planning aid, not a substitute for personalised professional advice. Every calculator is built to be accurate for the general case, but your specific situation can have details a general tool can't account for. See the Disclaimer for the full picture.
