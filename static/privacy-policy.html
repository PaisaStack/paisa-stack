This policy is a template written for how Paisa Stack currently operates. If you add new tools, collect new kinds of data, or start using new third-party services, review and update this page — and consider having a lawyer check it once the site handles real payment volume.

What this site collects

The calculators on Paisa Stack (tax, EMI, HRA, and the rest) run entirely in your browser. The numbers you type in are never sent to our servers, stored, or seen by us — they exist only on your device while you're using the tool.

We do not currently use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts on this site[update this line if you add Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar].

What happens when you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp — to buy an ebook, book a consultation, or ask about a service — that conversation happens on WhatsApp's platform and is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We keep those conversations to respond to you and deliver what you've asked for (like sending an ebook PDF), and we don't sell or share your WhatsApp number with anyone else.

Payments

Payments for ebooks and consultations are processed through Razorpay [update once live]. We don't see or store your card, UPI, or bank details — that information is handled directly by Razorpay under their own security and privacy practices.

Free downloads (ebooks, trackers)

Free trackers can be downloaded directly with no email or personal information required. Some ebooks are delivered via WhatsApp after payment, as described above.
