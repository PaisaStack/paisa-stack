How this calculator works

Your offer letter shows CTC, but your bank account sees in-hand salary. The gap is usually 12% to 15% of CTC, more once income tax starts. This calculator works out that gap using the assumptions most offer letters don't spell out.

Enter three things:

  • Annual CTC: the total cost-to-company figure from your offer letter. If part of it is a yearly bonus, enter only the fixed part to see your monthly salary.
  • Basic salary (% of CTC): set to 50% by default, because the new labour codes require basic pay plus DA to be at least half of total pay. Older structures used 35% to 40%.
  • Other new-regime deductions: mainly your employer's NPS contribution under Section 80CCD(2). Leave it at ₹0 if you don't have one.

The formula

In-hand = CTC − employer PF − gratuity − employee PF − professional tax − income tax

  • Employer PF: 12% of basic. It goes to your PF account, not your salary.
  • Gratuity: 4.81% of basic (15 days' pay for each year, divided over 26 working days). You only receive it after 5 years of service.
  • Employee PF: 12% of basic, cut from your salary every month.
  • Professional tax: ₹200 a month. It's zero in states like Delhi and Haryana and about ₹2,500 a year in Maharashtra.
  • Income tax: new regime slabs on gross salary minus the ₹75,000 standard deduction, plus 4% cess. Taxable income up to ₹12 lakh is fully rebated.

When income tax starts

With basic at 50%, your tax stays zero up to a CTC of about ₹13.9 lakh. Above that, it rises quickly. At ₹20 lakh CTC, gross salary is ₹18,31,900, taxable income is ₹17,56,900 and tax with cess is ₹1,57,435. That leaves about ₹1,29,339 a month in hand. Compare both regimes in the Income Tax calculator before you tell HR which one to use.

What this calculator doesn't include

  • PF on the wage ceiling: some employers cut PF only on the statutory ceiling, now ₹25,000 a month (from 17 September 2026). That makes PF ₹3,000 a month and in-hand higher than shown.
  • Variable pay, joining bonus and insurance: these sit inside CTC but aren't paid monthly. Remove them from CTC before you enter it.
  • Old regime: HRA, 80C and 80D only apply in the old regime. Use the HRA calculator and the Income Tax calculator for that comparison.

Frequently asked questions

What is the in-hand salary for a ₹12 lakh CTC?

About ₹85,395 a month with basic at 50% of CTC and PF on the full basic. Income tax is zero under the new regime because taxable income is about ₹10.24 lakh, below the ₹12 lakh rebate limit.

Why is my in-hand salary lower than CTC divided by 12?

CTC includes employer PF and gratuity, which never reach your monthly salary. Your own 12% PF, professional tax and TDS are then deducted from what is left. Together these usually take 12% to 15% of CTC before any income tax.

What basic salary percentage should I enter?

50% for most offers from 2026. The labour codes in force since 21 November 2025 require basic pay plus DA to be at least half of total pay, so most companies now set basic at 50% of CTC. Check your offer letter if you're unsure.

Is employee PF deducted from taxable income?

Not in the new regime, which this calculator uses. Section 80C, which covers your own PF contribution, is only available in the old regime. Employer PF up to 12% of basic is not taxed in your hands.

What should I enter in the other deductions box?

Only deductions the new regime allows, mainly your employer's NPS contribution under Section 80CCD(2), up to 14% of basic. Don't enter 80C, 80D or HRA here; those only work in the old regime.

Does the calculator include the ₹25,000 PF wage ceiling?

No. It cuts PF on your full basic. If your employer cuts PF only on the ₹25,000 statutory ceiling (effective 17 September 2026), your PF is ₹3,000 a month and your in-hand salary will be higher than shown.
