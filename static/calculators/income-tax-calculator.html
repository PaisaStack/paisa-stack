How this works

India's new tax regime offers lower slab rates but almost no exemptions. The old regime keeps HRA, 80C, 80D, and home loan interest deductions, but at higher rates. Which one wins depends entirely on your specific numbers — there's no universal answer, which is exactly why guessing costs people real money every year.
