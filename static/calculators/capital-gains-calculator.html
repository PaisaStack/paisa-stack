How this works

Capital gains tax rules vary sharply by asset type: equity has a 12-month long-term threshold and a ₹1.25 lakh annual exemption; debt funds are taxed at your slab rate regardless of holding period; property and gold have their own 24-month threshold and indexation rules that changed materially in 2024.
