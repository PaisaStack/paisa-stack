Black garlic making: free business guide

Black garlic is regular garlic slowly aged at 60-75°C for 2-4 weeks until the cloves turn black, soft and sweet. Very few Indian producers make it, and it sells as a gourmet and health food. This 2-page guide shows you how to start.

What's inside

  • Investment: about ₹1-2 lakh, with a line-by-line cost table (fermenter, raw garlic, packaging, registrations).
  • Step-by-step setup: choosing the machine and garlic, test batches, drying, packing and value-added products.
  • Licences: FSSAI, Udyam, GST and trademark, and when each one applies.
  • Costing and margin: input cost per kg, wholesale and retail prices, and typical gross margin.
  • First customers: gourmet stores, marketplaces, gyms, chefs and export buyers.
  • Risks and a simple plan for your first 30-45 days.

Costs and prices in the guide are indicative 2026 estimates and vary by city, scale and supplier. Verify quotes and local rules before investing.
